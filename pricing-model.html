What the model is guessing

There have been no sales at any price. 54 businesses emailed, 8 opened their site, 4 reached the pricing, 0 bought. With 0 out of 54, the honest range for the true close rate is anywhere from 0% to about 5.6%. That is too wide to tell you whether $750 is wrong.

So this is not a forecast. It converts a close rate into money. You supply the close rate, and right now nobody — not you, not me — knows what it is. The one way to find out is to send enough emails at one price to get a real number.

Churn is assumed at 3% a month, meaning an average customer stays about 33 months. Nothing has been observed, because nothing has been sold.
