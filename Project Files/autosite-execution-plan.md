### Week 2: Build the Sample Websites

Pick 20 businesses from the list. Randomize across categories (plumbing, dental, cleaning, restaurants, etc.).

For each one, use Claude Code or Lovable to generate a 5-page website:
- Home (hero + services + call to action)
- Services/About (2–3 sections specific to their business)
- Contact
- Maybe a gallery if relevant

The websites should be:
- Fast (Vercel-hosted)
- Mobile-friendly
- Branded with their business name, colors, phone number
- Each hosted at: `[businessname]-preview.yoursite.com`

**Cost:** $0 in tools. Your time: ~60 hours total (3 hours per site). Use this as a template library for everything after.

**Tool:** Claude Code or Lovable or bolt.diy (whichever you prefer)

### Week 3: Test the Campaign (Small Mailer)

Order 500 postcards from Vistaprint or a local print vendor ($200–300 total).

The postcard says:

```
[Front]
=================================
We Built You A Website.
See your free preview:
yoursite.com/[businessname]-preview
=================================

[Back]
Your business deserves customers who can find you online.

We're [Your Name], and I hand-built a website for your [Business Type].
Check it out. If you love it, let's make it official.

[Your Phone Number]
[Simple CTA]: "Yes, I want this"
```

Send these 500 to random businesses from your list. Include a unique tracking URL or phone number so you can measure response.

**Cost:** $200–300 for postcards + $50 for tracking setup. **Total: $350**

**Timeline:** Week 3, you mail them. By end of week 4, responses start coming in.

### Week 4: Conversion & Iteration

You'll probably get 10–20 leads from 500 postcards (2–4% response rate). Of those, 2–5 will actually call or click through.

Do NOT build a complicated sales process. Pick up the phone. Say:

*"Hi [Name], this is Anan. I built you a free website preview. Did you get a chance to look at it? [If yes] Cool — it's yours for $1,500, or I can make changes. What would make it perfect? [If no] No problem. The website is live at [URL]. Give it a look and text me if you want to move forward."*

Close at least one customer this week. Get payment via Stripe. Refine their website based on feedback. Deliver it.

**Expected outcome:** 1–3 paying customers, $1,500–$4,500 in revenue.

## Part 6: The Tools (Exact Stack for AutoSite)

### Lead Generation & Automation
- **SunBiz database:** Florida Secretary of State (free, public records)
- **Google Maps API:** For business verification and website detection ($0–100/month for your scale)
- **n8n:** Workflow orchestration to tie it together (you already know this)

- **Output:** CSV file with target businesses

### Website Building
- **Claude Code:** For bespoke builds using HTML + Tailwind + Next.js
- **Lovable:** If you want drag-and-drop speed
- **bolt.diy:** For quick, dirt-simple sites
- **Vercel:** Hosting (free tier is fine, $20/month when you scale)

### Direct Mail Campaign
- **Vistaprint:** Postcard printing ($200–500 per 500 cards)
- **LocallyOptimized or PostGrid:** Print + mail automation (if you scale beyond manual ordering)
- **Tracking:** Google Forms + unique URLs per business to measure responses

### Payment Collection
- **Stripe:** Credit card processing (2.9% + $0.30 per transaction)
- **Stripe Invoicing:** Send them an invoice, they pay online

### CRM (Super Light)
- **Airtable or NocoDB:** Track leads, conversions, projects in flight
- **Google Sheets:** If you want to keep it stupid simple

**Total monthly burn:** ~$100 (Vercel + Maps API + Airtable). Everything else is pay-as-you-go.
